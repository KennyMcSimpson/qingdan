## 中文

这次把轻单做得更适合每天用：循环小事只需要设置一次，窗口会主动让开工作区，常驻提醒也终于有了固定的位置。

- **循环提醒**：每天、工作日（周一至周五）、每周，按本机时间选择提醒时刻。完成本次会留下记录并进入下一次；新周期自动更新，错过的不会堆积。
- **贴边标签**：闲置时自动收成 46 像素宽的标签，点击展开。可在设置里关闭自动贴边，也可以手动收起、收成小条或隐藏 15 分钟。
- **顶部常驻区**：常驻提醒固定在全部清单顶部，可折叠；默认展示两条，其余一键查看。标为重要的优先显示。
- **更紧凑的界面**：循环有独立分类，小窗口里的列表留有更多空间。编辑或选择备份时不会自动收起。
- 兼容 1.0 的清单、备注和外观设置，仍使用 `%APPDATA%\Qingdan`。更新前建议导出备份，然后完整解压新版程序包。

下载 `Qingdan-1.1.0-windows-x64.zip`，解压整个文件夹后双击 `Qingdan.exe`。无需安装开发环境。

提醒需要程序运行；退出或关机后不会唤醒。工作日指周一到周五，不识别法定节假日。完成记录可清理，刚完成的一次可撤销。

## English

A more practical everyday Qingdan: set recurring tasks once, let the window move out of your way, and keep persistent reminders in a predictable place.

- **Recurring reminders**: daily, weekdays (Monday–Friday), and weekly, at a chosen local time. Completion records the current occurrence and schedules the next. Missed periods roll forward without accumulating tasks.
- **Edge tab**: automatically tucks into a 46-pixel-wide tab when you switch away. Click to expand; automatic tucking is optional. Manual tuck, compact strip, and a 15-minute hide are also available.
- **Top reminder shelf**: persistent reminders stay above the scrolling list. Collapse the shelf, see the first two, or open the full collection. Important reminders come first.
- **Denser layout**: a separate recurring category, more usable space in small windows, and protection against auto-tucking while editing or choosing backup files.
- Reads existing 1.0 tasks and preferences from the same `%APPDATA%\Qingdan` folder. Export a backup before updating, then extract the complete new package.

Download `Qingdan-1.1.0-windows-x64.zip`, extract everything, and launch `Qingdan.exe`. No development tools required. The application UI is currently Chinese.

Reminders require the app to remain running. Weekdays do not include a public-holiday calendar. Completed occurrences can be cleared; the latest completion can be undone.
